Return & Refund Policy

At ANIM Clothing, customer satisfaction is our priority. If you are not fully satisfied with your purchase, you may request a return or exchange under the following conditions:

Returns

We offer a 3-day return policy, which means you have 3 days after receiving your item to request a return.

To be eligible for a return:

  • Item must be unused, unworn, and in original condition
  • Tags and packaging must be intact
  • Proof of purchase (order number or receipt) is required

Non-Returnable Items:

  • Sale or discounted items
  • Customized or made-to-order products

Exchanges

We only replace items if:

  • You received the wrong item
  • The item is defective
  • You need a size exchange (subject to availability)

Return Process

To start a return or exchange:

  • Call / WhatsApp: 0321-1156808
  • Provide your order number and reason for return

Our team will guide you through the next steps.

Shipping Policy

At Anim Clothing, we are committed to delivering your orders quickly and efficiently.

Order Processing

All orders are processed within 24 hours of confirmation. Orders placed on weekends or public holidays will be processed on the next working day.

Delivery Time Frames

  • Same City Delivery: Orders within the same city are delivered within 24 to 48 hours.
  • Other Cities & Provinces: Orders shipped to other cities and provinces are delivered within 3 to 5 business days.

Shipping Charges

Shipping charges, if applicable, will be calculated and displayed at checkout.

Order Tracking

Once your order has been dispatched, you will receive a tracking number via email or SMS to monitor your shipment status.

Delivery Delays

While we strive to meet the stated delivery timelines, delays may occur due to unforeseen circumstances such as weather conditions, public holidays, or courier service issues.
